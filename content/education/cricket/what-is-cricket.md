Cricket is played between two teams of eleven players. One team bats,
trying to score as many runs as possible before losing its batters; the
other team bowls and fields, trying to restrict those runs and get
batters out. The teams then swap roles. How many times each team bats,
and for how long, depends on the [match format](#match-format) — a
Twenty20 match finishes in a few hours, a Test match can run for five
days.
