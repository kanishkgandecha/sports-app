Before a match starts, the two captains toss a coin. Whoever wins gets to
choose: bat first, or bowl first. The choice usually comes down to pitch
and weather conditions — a pitch expected to get harder to bat on as the
match goes on favours batting first, while overcast conditions that help
the ball swing favour bowling first. This is the first real decision of
the match, and it's why you'll see a "won the toss, chose to bat/bowl"
line before any runs are scored.
