A wicket falls when a batter gets out. The most common ways: bowled (the
ball hits the stumps), caught (a fielder catches the ball before it
touches the ground after the batter hit it), leg before wicket, or run
out (the fielding side breaks the stumps before the batter completes a
run). Each team has ten wickets to lose before an innings ends — the
scoreline "167/6" means 167 runs for the loss of 6 wickets, with four
batters left to come.
