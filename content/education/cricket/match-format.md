The three formats you'll see are Twenty20 (T20), One Day International
(ODI), and Test cricket. In T20 and ODI matches, each team bats once,
for a fixed number of overs (20 for T20, 50 for an ODI); the side that
scores more runs wins. Test cricket has no over limit — each team bats
twice across up to five days, and a result depends on both sides
completing their innings before time runs out, or a team scores more
runs than the other could ever catch up to.
