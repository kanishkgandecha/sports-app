Run rate is simply runs divided by overs faced — a team on 90 runs after
15 overs has a run rate of 6.0. It's the pace of scoring. When a team is
batting second and chasing a target, you'll also see a required run
rate: the rate they now need to maintain, over their remaining overs, to
reach the target before running out of overs. A required run rate
climbing well above the current run rate is a sign the chasing team is
falling behind the pace they need.
