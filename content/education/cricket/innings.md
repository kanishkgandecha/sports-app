An innings is one team's turn at batting. It ends when the team has
either lost enough [wickets](#wicket) that too few batters remain (ten
in an eleven-a-side team), or — in T20 and ODI cricket — when they've
faced their allotted number of [overs](#over). In a two-innings match
(T20, ODI), the team batting second is chasing a target set by the
team that batted first, and you'll see a required run rate once that
chase is underway. Test matches give each side two innings.
