  the season into the Drivers' and Constructors' standings.

Every Grand Prix awards points to the top ten finishers — 25 for first
place, down to 1 for tenth — plus a bonus point for the fastest lap if the
driver who set it finishes in the top ten. A driver's points add up across
every race weekend into the Drivers' Championship; a team's points are the
combined total of both its drivers, added up the same way into the
Constructors' Championship. These two standings run in parallel all season,
and the driver or team with the most points when the season ends is
champion.
