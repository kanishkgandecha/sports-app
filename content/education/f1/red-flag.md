A red flag is a full stop: it's shown when a hazard is too severe to manage
under a Safety Car or VSC — heavy debris, poor visibility, or a serious
incident. All cars slow down and either return to the pit lane or form up on
the grid, and the clock stops until race control is ready to resume.
