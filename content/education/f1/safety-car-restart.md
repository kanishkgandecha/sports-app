Once the hazard is cleared, the Safety Car peels into the pit lane at the
end of a lap. The leader controls the pace from there and can restart
racing at any point once past the Safety Car line — which makes the restart
itself one of the most contested, overtaking-heavy moments of a race, since
everyone is bunched together at once for the only time all race.
