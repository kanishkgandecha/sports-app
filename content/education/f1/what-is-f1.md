Formula 1 is a championship, not a single race. Ten teams (called
constructors) each field two drivers across a season of roughly 24 [Grand
Prix](#grand-prix) weekends worldwide. Every race weekend awards points
based on finishing position, and those points accumulate into two
championships run in parallel: one for drivers, one for constructors.
