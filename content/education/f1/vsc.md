The VSC does the same job as a full Safety Car — neutralizing the race after
an on-track hazard — without a car actually going out. Instead, every driver
must keep their lap time above a minimum "delta" the whole way around,
enforced by their pit wall and the FIA's timing data. It's used for hazards
that can be cleared quickly, since restarting from a VSC is faster than
gathering the field behind a physical car.
