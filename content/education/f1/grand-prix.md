A Grand Prix is one stop on the season, run across a Friday-to-Sunday
weekend at a single circuit. Most weekends follow the same shape: one or
more practice sessions, a qualifying session that sets the starting grid,
and the race itself — though some weekends add a Sprint and Sprint
Qualifying session earlier in the schedule.
