When something happens on track that's dangerous to leave under green-flag
racing — debris, a stopped car, marshals working near the racing line — race
control deploys the Safety Car. It pulls out in front of the leader, and the
whole field bunches up behind it in position order at reduced speed. No
overtaking is allowed while it's out, except to let lapped cars unlap
themselves in some situations.

Gaps between cars collapse to almost nothing during a Safety Car period,
which is exactly why teams often use it to make a "free" pit stop — the time
lost by stopping is much smaller than it would be at racing speed.
