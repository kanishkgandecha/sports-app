Flags are how race control tells every driver what's happening on track in
real time, since radio messages alone would be too slow and too easy to
miss. A yellow flag means slow down and don't overtake in that section; a
green flag clears it; a blue flag tells a driver being lapped to let a
faster car through. When a situation is serious enough, it escalates beyond
a flag shown at one marshal post into a full [Safety Car](#safety-car),
[Virtual Safety Car](#vsc), or [Red Flag](#red-flag) for the whole track.
